The flow in pictures (doc-cards and card)

A page opens with how the thing is used, in three to five pictures.

The number is a badge, the heading a card-title, the aside a doc-note.

2Press

3Check

What changed is drawn in the doc-hi colour.

The long steps and the terms (folded: collapse)
  1. Long steps and a glossary come after the flow, folded.
  2. Specification (what it does not do, only when, since which version) stays in the reference, off the page.

A page of prose

Prose says in its first lines what the reader takes away.

Part: doc-prose

An explanation or a report written as Markdown is read in order, top to bottom. Write the plain tags and they look like this. A link is underlined, in the primary colour.
